  • The public 2026 schedule has later material beyond the supplied decks. Navigation, detailed networking, persistence, animations and deployment are supplementary to the supplied lectures and are not confirmed assessment requirements.

  • Course demo destinations were verified; their interactive contents were not run. Some lecture examples exist as images in slides and should be visually inspected in addition to text extraction.
  • The supplied Interview 1 rules in Lecture 4a prohibit homework solution-like code and most AI use during the interview. The generated solution material is for preparation; actual assessment rules and scope should be confirmed with staff.
